# Scenario trajectory clustering methodology

Inputs are the validated `outputs/trajectory_analysis/scenario_*/raw_trajectory_samples.csv` files, conflict-zone JSON/event CSV files, processing manifests, and `C:\Users\patl5\Downloads\XCPedestrian-GroundTruth-RERUN.xlsx`. Pedestrian position is B VR Pos X/Z; avatar position is never used.

Paths are restricted to -3 to +5 seconds around the first actor's zone arrival, translated to the shared scenario zone center, rotated toward positive pedestrian travel, and resampled to 50 equal cumulative-distance increments. Path clustering uses average-linkage agglomeration on pairwise RMS spatial distance. Response clustering uses Ward linkage on compact robustly standardized timing, speed, stopping, distance, and PET features. Candidate k is 2–4; acceptance requires minimum cluster size 3, mean subsample ARI at least 0.70, and leave-one-feature-out ARI at least 0.70 where applicable. Seed=42; bootstrap repetitions=50.

For Scenarios 12 and 16, t=0 uses the first pedestrian entry or explicitly modeled counterfactual vehicle arrival in the existing `turn_uncertainty` zone. This is labeled as a fallback/counterfactual anchor and is not an observed vehicle-zone entry. A trial with neither arrival is excluded rather than aligned to closest approach. Speed uses the upstream centered nine-sample smoothing. Missing numeric features are median-imputed only within scenario by the robust scaler; labels remain missing. Ground truth is joined after clustering and is never used as an unsupervised feature. Cross-scenario clustering uses within-scenario standardized behavioral summaries and explicitly excludes raw coordinates. Results are exploratory; repeated participants, small groups, modeled uncertainty arrivals, missing annotations, and sensitivity to window/feature choices limit generalization.
